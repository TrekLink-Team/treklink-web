## Facts confirmed

- `@ScheduledJob` takes a fixed cron, and the parameter's lower bound is 5 s, so the job ticks every
  5 s and skips a tick earlier than `monitoring.staleSweepSeconds` (1 s tolerance).
- One run at a time is the job runner's `pg_try_advisory_xact_lock`; the mark insert
  (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) and the guarded delete make a second concurrent sweep a no-op (db-spec).
- Every `alert_deliveries` row needs a `transitionId`, and `AlertDispatcherJob` sends MSG31 ("SOS ...
  Acknowledge now.") for every row, so the stale-flag alert of incidents design §3 cannot be written
  without a ruling. Left open and asked.
- The incident already keeps its last position and fix time (`lastPositionAt`) from the episode, so the
  flag stores nothing more; the read models expose `lastPosition` without its time.
