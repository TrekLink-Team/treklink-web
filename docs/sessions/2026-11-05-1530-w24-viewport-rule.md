## Reading taken (question sent to the leader)

`#main` stays the one vertical scroll region of a standard page, with the page title, the command bar and
the action bar pinned in it; fit pages (`data-fit-page`) take exactly the content area and scroll one
inner region (the maps, the permission grids). The strict reading (`#main` never scrolls, every page split
into header, body and bar) was asked as a `QUESTION FOR THE LEADER`; it would touch about 45 page roots,
teammates' pages among them.

## W24b: the strict reading (leader, 13:05 and 13:45; chapter 06 §11.1)

`#main` is not a scroll region any more. Built on `fix/viewport-no-page-scroll` on top of `39f5f865`,
without rebasing the sealed commits.

- `CollectionView` fills a fit page when it is a direct child of a `data-fit-page` element (Tailwind
  variants `cv-fill-root:` and `cv-fill:`): the command bar, the bulk bar and the pager stay, the table
  box scrolls with its header row sticky, the phone card list scrolls, the details pane scrolls inside
  itself. Elsewhere (contract home tabs, invoice lines, the overview) a CollectionView keeps its natural
  height. `TabPanel fill` makes a tab panel such an element.
- `PageScrollBody` (in `PageChrome.tsx`) is the record, form or multi-list body between the pinned
  header and the action bar. `useScrollStop` gives a region with nothing focusable a tab stop and a name.
- List pages made fit: devices, incidents (organization, Staff queue and authority reports), contracts,
  contract approval, invoices, billing (invoices beside the open invoice, payments), members, API keys,
  field stations (both), loss cases, service credits, damage charges, accounts, variants, audit log,
  sign-in events, compliance cases, maintenance (three tabs), organization review. Body pages: parameters,
  sync status, prices, reports, system health, telemetry, organization overview, stock-take, roster, roles
  on phones, contract home (the lifecycle card moves into the body below 600 px), device, incident (both),
  invoice, contract request, counter handover and return counter, payment, demo controls.
