# 2026-11-05 15:30 (UTC+7): W24 viewport rule, starting with the live map

KhoaDD under D-078, branch `fix/viewport-no-page-scroll` from `dev` at `40de81b5`. Frontend and specs.
Rule: leader, 2026-11-05 (screen never scrolls by itself), checks as revised by chapter 06 §9 to §11
(treklink-docs#170). Written into `specs/frontend/ui-design/0-ui-standard.md` §7 and `design.md` §7.

## Reading taken (question sent to the leader)

`#main` stays the one vertical scroll region of a standard page, with the page title, the command bar and
the action bar pinned in it; fit pages (`data-fit-page`) take exactly the content area and scroll one
inner region (the maps, the permission grids). The strict reading (`#main` never scrolls, every page split
into header, body and bar) was asked as a `QUESTION FOR THE LEADER`; it would touch about 45 page roots,
teammates' pages among them.

## Environment

Postgres 16 `initdb` cluster on port 5433, database `treklink`, all migrations, `seed:demo` (56 row
groups); `MQTT_INGESTION_ENABLED=false`, no broker. The baseline ran on a `git worktree` of `origin/dev`
served by a second Vite server (port 5174), so the live edits never touched it. Chromium from
`/opt/pw-browsers`.

## Audit

Every route of `router.tsx` with a seeded id (incident, contract, device, invoice), signed in as Admin,
Staff, Org Manager and Org Operator, plus `/plans`, `/sign-in`, `/register` and `/forgot` signed out; at
1440x900, 1280x720, 900x800, 360x740, 320x568 and 318x236 (400 % zoom); Canopy and Volt. Per view:
`document.scrollingElement` and `#main` (scrollHeight and scrollWidth against client size, 1 px slack),
every overflowing descendant (nested scroll, keyboard reachability), and `#main` scrolled to its end with
the `h1` and `[data-page-actions]` checked inside its box. 600 views each run, one screenshot per view.

At 318x236 the document scrolls on all 100 views after the change; that is the 400 % release the rule
allows (before, `#main` was 96 px tall there).

Looked at by eye: contract home at 360 (light and dark, scrolled) and 1280, invoice detail at 1440 dark
(scrolled), permission grid at 1280, live map at 360, 900 and 1440, device detail at 360 (scrolled), plans
at 320, 360 and 1440 (scrolled), field stations at 320, incidents at 318x236, and contact sheets of every
route at 360 px dark for the four roles and at 320 px light for Staff. Axe (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA tags)

`/t/fleet` 360, `/o/contracts/:id` 360, `/o/invoices/:id` 1280: 0 of 92 focus stops hidden.

## Findings written down

- `Badge` measured 22.0 CSS px high (`/t/fleet`, 1440): under the 24 px target of the interactive status
  badge (0-ui-standard §3).
- A visually hidden element is absolutely positioned and escapes a scroll box with no positioned ancestor
  (`/register` document, map panel at 320): 0-ui-standard §7.
- `usePublishedHeight` observed the content box, so a compact header that gains padding kept its old
  height; it now observes the border box.
