All three are provided: figure 11 is the Rental FSM designed for this request, figure 13 is the graded Device FSM. The SDD should present 12 and 13 as the graded pair and may add 11.
2. MF-01 and MF-03 activity diagrams are split into parts joined by connector nodes because a single swimlane prints near 3 pt. Place the parts consecutively.
